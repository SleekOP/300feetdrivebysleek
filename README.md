# 300 Feet — Dhaka Road Run

Take a fast drive along Dhaka’s 300 Feet Road. Dodge traffic, watch your speed, and try to reach Neela Market before the police catch you. The destination is 2 km away.

## How to play

- **Up / W:** Accelerate
- **Down / S:** Brake
- **Left / Right / A / D:** Steer
- **C** or the camera button: switch between cockpit and chase views
- On your phone, rotate to landscape and use the on-screen arrow controls.

Choose **The Run** for a police chase, or **Free Drive** to keep cruising. Use the in-game menu to switch modes. The sun/moon button changes between day and night, and the rain button toggles rain.

## Radio and sound

Turn on Radio Foorti 88.0 FM for its live broadcast. The radio needs an internet connection and may be unavailable if the station’s stream is down. The sound button controls the engine, siren, and crash effects separately.

## Designed for desktop and mobile

On desktop, use the keyboard controls above. On a phone, rotate to landscape to play. The game will ask you to rotate if your phone is in portrait mode.
